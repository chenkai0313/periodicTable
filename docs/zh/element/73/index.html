Tantalum 钽

Tantalum is a Transition Metals element with the symbol Ta and atomic number 73. Located in period 6, group 5 (d-block), it has an atomic weight of 180.95 u and is classified as a solid at standard temperature and pressure. First identified in 1802 by Anders Gustav Ekeberg in Sweden. Tantalum has an electron configuration of [Xe] 4f¹⁴ 5d³ 6s² with 6 electron shells containing 2, 8, 18, 32, 11, 2 electrons respectively. It is commonly used in: capacitors in phones, surgical implants, alloys.

钽是一种过渡金属元素，符号为Ta，原子序数为73。位于第6周期，第5族（d区），原子量为180.95 u，在标准温度和压力下为固体。于1802 年首次被确认，由Anders Gustav Ekeberg发现。钽的电子排布为[Xe] 4f¹⁴ 5d³ 6s²，具有6个电子层，分别含有2、8、18、32、11、2个电子。常见用途包括：手机电容，外科植入物，合金。

Transition Metals过渡金属

Hard, dense metals with multiple oxidation states. Often colorful compounds.

Softer, lower-melting metals between transition and metalloid.

Semiconductors bridging metals and nonmetals. Basis of modern electronics.

Poor conductors, essential for life (C, N, O, P, S). Form covalent bonds.
