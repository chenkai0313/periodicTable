Rb

Rubidium 铷

Rubidium is a Alkali Metals element with the symbol Rb and atomic number 37. Located in period 5, group 1 (s-block), it has an atomic weight of 85.468 u and is classified as a solid at standard temperature and pressure. First identified in 1861 by Robert Bunsen in Germany. Rubidium has an electron configuration of [Kr] 5s¹ with 5 electron shells containing 2, 8, 18, 8, 1 electrons respectively. It is commonly used in: atomic clocks, fireworks, research, glass.

铷是一种碱金属元素，符号为Rb，原子序数为37。位于第5周期，第1族（s区），原子量为85.468 u，在标准温度和压力下为固体。于1861 年首次被确认，由Robert Bunsen发现。铷的电子排布为[Kr] 5s¹，具有5个电子层，分别含有2、8、18、8、1个电子。常见用途包括：原子钟，烟花，研究，玻璃。

Element Categories元素分类说明

Alkali Metals碱金属

Highly reactive, soft metals with 1 valence electron. Explosive in water.

高度活泼的软金属，1个价电子，遇水爆炸。

Alkaline Earth碱土金属

Reactive metals with 2 valence electrons. Burns with bright colors.

较活泼金属，2个价电子，燃烧产生明亮颜色。

Transition Metals过渡金属

Hard, dense metals with multiple oxidation states. Often colorful compounds.

坚硬致密金属，多变氧化态，化合物常呈彩色。

Post-Transition后过渡金属

Softer, lower-melting metals between transition and metalloid.

较软的金属，熔点较低，介于过渡金属和准金属之间。

Metalloids准金属

Semiconductors bridging metals and nonmetals. Basis of modern electronics.

半导体特性，介于金属与非金属之间，现代电子学基础。

Nonmetals非金属

Poor conductors, essential for life (C, N, O, P, S). Form covalent bonds.

不良导体，生命必需元素(C,N,O,P,S)，形成共价键。

Halogens卤素

Highly reactive nonmetals forming salts with metals. "Salt former".

极活泼非金属，与金属形成盐类，"成盐元素"。

Noble Gases稀有气体

Colorless, odorless inert gases. Full valence shell — chemically stable.

无色无味惰性气体，满价电子层，化学性质稳定。

Lanthanides镧系元素

Silvery rare-earth metals. Used in magnets, lasers, and phosphors.

银白色稀土金属，用于磁铁、激光和荧光粉。

Actinides锕系元素

Mostly radioactive heavy elements. Includes nuclear fuels U and Pu.

大多为放射性重元素，包含核燃料铀和钚。
