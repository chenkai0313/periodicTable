Darmstadtium 𫟼

Darmstadtium is a Transition Metals element with the symbol Ds and atomic number 110. Located in period 7, group 10 (d-block), it has an atomic weight of 281 u and is classified as a synthetic at standard temperature and pressure. First identified in 1994 by Sigurd Hofmann in Germany. Darmstadtium has an electron configuration of [Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d⁸ 7s² with 7 electron shells containing 2, 8, 18, 32, 32, 16, 2 electrons respectively. It is commonly used in: research only.

𫟼是一种过渡金属元素，符号为Ds，原子序数为110。位于第7周期，第10族（d区），原子量为281 u，在标准温度和压力下为人造元素。于1994 年首次被确认，由Sigurd Hofmann发现。𫟼的电子排布为[Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d⁸ 7s²，具有7个电子层，分别含有2、8、18、32、32、16、2个电子。常见用途包括：仅用于研究。
