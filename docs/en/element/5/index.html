Boron 硼

Boron is a Metalloids element with the symbol B and atomic number 5. Located in period 2, group 13 (p-block), it has an atomic weight of 10.81 u and is classified as a solid at standard temperature and pressure. First identified in 1808 by Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac in France. Boron has an electron configuration of [He] 2s² 2p¹ with 2 electron shells containing 2, 3 electrons respectively. It is commonly used in: borosilicate glass, detergents, semiconductors, borax.

硼是一种准金属元素，符号为B，原子序数为5。位于第2周期，第13族（p区），原子量为10.81 u，在标准温度和压力下为固体。于1808 年首次被确认，由Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac发现。硼的电子排布为[He] 2s² 2p¹，具有2个电子层，分别含有2、3个电子。常见用途包括：硼硅玻璃，洗涤剂，半导体，硼砂。

Element Categories元素分类说明

Alkali Metals碱金属

Highly reactive, soft metals with 1 valence electron. Explosive in water.

高度活泼的软金属，1个价电子，遇水爆炸。

Alkaline Earth碱土金属

Reactive metals with 2 valence electrons. Burns with bright colors.

较活泼金属，2个价电子，燃烧产生明亮颜色。

Transition Metals过渡金属

Hard, dense metals with multiple oxidation states. Often colorful compounds.

坚硬致密金属，多变氧化态，化合物常呈彩色。

Post-Transition后过渡金属

Softer, lower-melting metals between transition and metalloid.

较软的金属，熔点较低，介于过渡金属和准金属之间。

Metalloids准金属

Semiconductors bridging metals and nonmetals. Basis of modern electronics.

半导体特性，介于金属与非金属之间，现代电子学基础。

Nonmetals非金属

Poor conductors, essential for life (C, N, O, P, S). Form covalent bonds.

不良导体，生命必需元素(C,N,O,P,S)，形成共价键。

Halogens卤素

Highly reactive nonmetals forming salts with metals. "Salt former".

极活泼非金属，与金属形成盐类，"成盐元素"。

Noble Gases稀有气体

Colorless, odorless inert gases. Full valence shell — chemically stable.

无色无味惰性气体，满价电子层，化学性质稳定。

Lanthanides镧系元素

Silvery rare-earth metals. Used in magnets, lasers, and phosphors.

银白色稀土金属，用于磁铁、激光和荧光粉。
