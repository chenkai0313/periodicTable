About PeriodicTable 关于 PeriodicTable

Last updated: 2026-05-14 最后更新：2026-05-14

Our Mission

PeriodicTable was built with a simple belief: understanding the elements should be intuitive, visual, and enjoyable. Whether you are a high school student preparing for an exam, a university researcher looking up quick reference data, or just someone curious about what makes up the universe — this site is for you.

We combine meticulously curated data from authoritative sources with interactive tools that make learning chemistry feel more like exploration than memorization.

Our Data Sources

  • PubChem — NIH
  • NIST
  • Royal Society of Chemistry
  • IUPAC Technical Reports

我们的使命

PeriodicTable 基于一个简单的信念而构建：理解元素应该是直观、可视化且令人愉悦的。无论你是备考的高中生、需要快速查阅参考数据的大学研究人员，还是对宇宙构成感到好奇的普通人——这个网站都为你而建。

我们将来自权威来源的精心校对数据与交互式工具相结合，使化学学习更像探索而非死记硬背。
